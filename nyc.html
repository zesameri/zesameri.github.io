Where to stay

North Brooklyn
Williamsburg or Greenpoint.
both gentrified. the latter is less convenient but classier.
Downtown Brooklyn
Brooklyn Heights, Fort Greene
stroller city, but best transit and historic.

Williamsburg / Greenpoint

before noon
Radio Bakery
Bakery · 135 India St, Greenpoint
weekday breakfast or lunch, avoid on weekends
Msgr. McGolrick Park
Park · Nassau Ave & Russell St, Greenpoint
really quaint park
Beacon's Closet
Thrift · 74 Guernsey St, Greenpoint
large curated thrift store.
Lunch & afternoon
Brooklyn Standard Deli
Deli · 188 Nassau Ave, Greenpoint
good sandwich deli
Taqueria Ramirez
Tacos · 94 Franklin St, Greenpoint
nice al pastor, the tortillas are okay
Caffè Panna
Italian ice cream · 16 Norman Ave, Greenpoint
fresh cream and olive oil with salt on ice cream. open Wed–Sun from 1:30pm.
after sunset
WNYC Transmitter Park
Park · Greenpoint Ave, Greenpoint
Waterfront park. nice sunset.
Uzuki
Soba · 95 Guernsey St, Greenpoint
get the tofu and soba, too expensive. the owner is really cool and makes the pottery
Paulie Gee's Slice Shop
Pizza · 110 Franklin St, Greenpoint
get the hellboy squared
Nighttime
Mr. Melo
Listening bar · 61 Withers St, Williamsburg
pretensious listening bar, chill lounge energy.
Good Room
Club · 98 Meserole Ave, Greenpoint
disco/house club

Downtown Brooklyn / Brooklyn Heights / Fort Greene

Miss Ada
New American · 184 Dekalb Ave, Fort Greene
my favorite restaurant in the city. reserve ahead or showup and wait
Speedy Romeo
Pizza · 376 Classon Ave, Clinton Hill
Really really good pizza and pasta, hipstery, not fancy, not divey.
Rhodora Wine Bar
Wine bar · 197 Adelphi St, Fort Greene
charming. natural wine, order at the bar, choose your seat.
Public Records
Bar/club · 233 Butler St, Gowanus
Very fun spot for house and some techno. check schedule.

Manhattan - East Chinatown / LES

Williamsburg Bridge
Bridge · Pedestrian / bike path
If you're heading between Brooklyn and LES/SOHO, cross via the Williamsburg Bridge by Citi Bike or on foot
Metrograph
Cinema/bar · 7 Ludlow St, LES
Really great selections of movies you may never see otherwise. Bar and restaurant upstairs.
Le Dive
Bar · 37 Canal St, LES
Pretentious ex-NYU kids in the bar around the corner that spills into the street.
Forever Taste
Dumplings · 27 Rutgers St, Chinatown
cash only hole in the wall with good dumplings and kids doing homework.
Seward Park
Park · Canal St & Essex St, LES
Watch older Chinese people play ping pong or do tai chi while eating messy dumplings.
